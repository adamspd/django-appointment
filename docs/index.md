**Django-Appointment**: simplifies appointment scheduling for a variety of projects. Built on Django, it's customizable, flexible, and ready to simplify your booking needs.

## The Why

Born from a [photography project](https://tchiiz.com/) struggling with email-based bookings. Existing solutions? Too niche (doctor/patient appointment, appointment module for a clinic etc...) or inflexible. So, I created a simple, adaptable solution for anyone needing appointment scheduling in their Django project.

## Take It For a Spin

Curious how it works? Check out the [live demo](https://django-appt.adamspierredavid.com/) and see Django-Appointment in action!
